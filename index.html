Education Technology · AI Platform

Elcrika Education Platform

Problem

Education is struggling to keep pace with the rapid growth of technology and AI. Classrooms still lean on a single teaching approach while every learner learns differently, leaving many unsupported — and generative AI is increasingly doing learners' work for them instead of building the critical thinking they'll actually need.

Engineering

We engineered Elcrika Education, an AI-powered ecosystem that identifies each learner's preferred style and tailors videos, podcasts, study plans, flashcards, and educational songs around it. The AI Tutor teaches through guided explanations rather than direct answers, while adaptive algorithms generate daily and weekly tasks from curriculum standards and past exam papers — alongside teacher classroom management, timetables, a National Digital Learning Library, career guidance, and real-time dashboards for parents, principals, and education authorities.

Impact

Schools embrace technology without replacing teachers. Learners build real understanding and independent thinking instead of AI-dependency, teachers reclaim time from admin, and parents and school leaders finally get real-time insight into progress — preparing the next generation for a technology-driven world instead of being left behind by it.

Sports & Fan Engagement

Urban Circle Sports

View Live Platform
Problem

Grassroots and university sport in South Africa has no centralized platform for managing competitions or showcasing athletes. Match data, statistics, fixtures, and standings are tracked manually, and fan engagement is scattered across social media — leaving talented football and netball players with little recognition beyond their local community, and even less chance of being discovered by professional clubs.

Engineering

We engineered Urban Circle Sports: a live match management and fan engagement platform digitizing grassroots competitions through real-time scores, standings, fixtures, and club administration, with digital player profiles, fan ratings and comments, live match centres, news, and galleries. Intelligent performance analytics aggregate fan ratings across every matchday into season rankings, turning standout performances into verified digital portfolios that coaches, scouts, and professional clubs can access directly.

Impact

Athletes gain continuous exposure beyond match day, supporters engage live through ratings and comments, and student administrators build real experience in sports management and digital media. Every performance becomes a permanent digital record — turning overlooked grassroots talent into pathways toward professional and international opportunity.
